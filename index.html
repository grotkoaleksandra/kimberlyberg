About Kimberly

I've been reading for people for over twenty years. Some come to me at a crossroads, some out of curiosity, some because something has gone quiet and they'd like to know why.

I don't do theatre and I don't do fear. I tell you what I get, plainly, and we talk it through together. You are welcome to bring questions, and you are just as welcome to bring none at all.

Readings happen by phone or video, wherever you are.

What a reading is

  1. 1

    If you'd like the session recorded, I'll send it to you afterwards.
